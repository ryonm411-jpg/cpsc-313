Quiz 3: The Plan

Nine short lessons built from your slides and the R3 practice questions.

Lessons

  1. Address bits and average access time. Hierarchy, locality, tag/index/offset. Prepares R3.3, R3.4, R3.6, R3.7, R3.8.
  2. Tracing and associativity. Hits and misses by hand, changing parameters. R3.5, R3.6.
  3. Replacement policies. Belady, LRU, LFU, MRU.
  4. Amdahl's Law and multi-level caches. R3.8, R3.9.
  5. Write policies. All four combinations and break-even rates. R3.10.
  6. Strided access. Row-major layout, trace reading, arrays bigger than the cache. R3.12, R3.13.
  7. MSI and MESI. R3.14, R3.15.
  8. Parallelism and hazard review. R3.2.
  9. Mock quiz. 50 minutes, all goals mixed.
  10. Targeted practice. Your mock-quiz misses, with new numbers.
  11. R3 weak spots. New variants of R3.4, 3.5, 3.10–3.15.
  12. Round two: A, E, F, G. Count the trips for writes, the set table for conflict misses, the four MESI rules, Flynn and locality.
  13. Retake replay. The real quiz questions Q3–Q10 from your quiz-viewing notes, each with a harder version and a drill.
